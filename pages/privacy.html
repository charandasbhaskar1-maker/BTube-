Privacy Policy

BTube Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

BTube is designed with privacy-first standards. We believe your entertainment data belongs solely to you.

1. Local Device Storage

Your channel customization (Display Name, Profile DP, Banner, Playlists, and Downloads) is saved securely directly inside your device's browser storage.

2. No Ad-Tracking or Data Selling

We do not track your off-app activity and we never sell your personal information to third-party ad networks.

3. Data Control

You can clear your entire watch history, offline downloads, and channel data anytime from the Settings screen.
