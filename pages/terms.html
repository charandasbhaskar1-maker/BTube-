Copyright Information & Fair Use

Creators must only upload content that they have created or are explicitly authorized to distribute. This includes audio tracks, instruments, background songs, and recorded footage.

Original Ownership Aapke dwara banaye gaye original lokgeet, banjo tutorial, bhajan aur vlogs par 100% intellectual property ka adhikar aapka rehta hai.
Music Labels & Commercial Audio Bina permission kisi music company ka original recorded master track use karne par copyright claim ya strike aa sakti hai.

Copyright Strikes Policy

BTube 3-Strikes Penalty Model follow karta hai taaki creators aur original rights-holders dono surakshit rahein:

Strike 1 1-week upload freeze. Live streaming & new videos band.
Strike 2 2-week freeze. Playlist & shorts creation restricted.
Strike 3 Permanent channel termination aur videos delete.
90 Days Expiration Rule Har copyright strike 90 dinon ke baad apne-aap expire ho jati hai agar aage koi naya violation na ho.
